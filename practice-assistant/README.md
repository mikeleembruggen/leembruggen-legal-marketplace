# Practice Assistant

AI matter management for small Australian law practices using **Microsoft 365** (Outlook + SharePoint) **or Google Workspace** (Gmail + Google Drive), running in Claude Cowork.

## What it does
- **Morning brief**: prioritised to-do list from the inbox, matched to matters
- **Matter dashboard**: one live page with today's actions, key dates, open matters, items awaiting reply and unfiled attachments
- **Deadline radar**, **weekly matter review** and other scheduled routines
- **Matter summaries**, **filing** into SharePoint or Google Drive, and **drafting from your precedents**

It never sends emails, never deletes files, and marks any calculated deadline "to verify".

## Getting started
After installing, click **+ New** in the left sidebar and say: **"Set up my practice assistant."** You'll start by giving your assistant a name. Setup takes about 45 minutes and walks you through everything.

## Requirements
- A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise) with Cowork
- Either Microsoft 365 (Outlook + SharePoint, Microsoft 365 connector) or Google Workspace (Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive connectors)

## Skills included
| Skill | Purpose |
|---|---|
| practice-setup | Guided setup, routines and settings changes |
| morning-triage | Daily inbox brief |
| matter-dashboard | HTML practice dashboard |
| matter-summary | Where a matter is at |
| file-to-matter | File emails and documents into SharePoint or Google Drive |
| draft-from-template | Draft from precedents; turn documents into templates |

## Important
This tool assists with organisation and drafting. It doesn't provide legal advice, and all deadlines, drafts and filings must be checked by the practitioner.
