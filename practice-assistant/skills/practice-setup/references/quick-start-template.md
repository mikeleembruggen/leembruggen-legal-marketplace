# Meet {{ASSISTANT_NAME}}: Quick Start

Open Claude (desktop app, browser or phone) and go into the **{{ASSISTANT_NAME}} – Practice Assistant** project. Your routines run by themselves on schedule, even when your computer is off. Otherwise, just ask in plain English.

## Your dashboard
> "Show my dashboard" / "Refresh the dashboard"

One page with today's actions, key dates for the next 30 days, open matters, and anything awaiting a reply or not yet filed. It refreshes automatically each weekday morning if you chose that routine.

## Your routines
See them under **Scheduled** in the left sidebar. To change one, just ask: "Move my morning brief to 8am" or "Add the weekly matter review."

## The five things you'll do most

**1. Start your day**
> "Morning brief"

You get a prioritised list: urgent today, this week, for info, and anything it couldn't match to a matter.

**2. Catch up on a matter**
> "Where are we at with Smith?"
> "Prep me for tomorrow's call on {{MATTER_EXAMPLE}}"

**3. File something**
> "File this email to the Smith matter"
> "Where should this attachment go?"

**4. Draft from your templates**
> "Draft an initial engagement letter for Smith"
> "Make this letter a template"

**5. Find anything**
> "Find the directions order in Smith"
> "What did the other side say about the offer?"

## What it will never do
- Send an email. It only drafts; you send.
- Delete or overwrite files without your say-so.
- Treat a deadline as settled. Anything it calculates is marked **to verify**.

## Good habits
- Put the **matter number in email subjects** (e.g. "{{MATTER_EXAMPLE}} – Response to offer"). Everything works better when it does.
- Add every new matter to the **Matter Register** when you open it.
- If something looks wrong, tell it: "That belongs to Jones, not Smith." It'll correct course.
